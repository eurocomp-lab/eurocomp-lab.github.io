Survey question repository

Before writing a single question, review existing, validated survey instruments. Using questions that have already been tested in comparable evaluations saves time, improves data quality, and makes findings comparable with evidence from other interventions and regions. This repository brings together questions drawn from established surveys (see Sources), organised by topic.

Topics

Firm and respondent profile

Company details

Name, registration number, sector, products, customers, year founded, location, website and social media.

Respondent details

Name, role, contact details, how they heard of the programme, gender, year of birth and education.

Company structure and ownership

Group membership, ownership structure, foreign ownership, boards and number of sites.

Company financial performance

Employees, turnover, wages, investment, exports, profit, costs, debt, equity and assets.

Thematic modules

Collaboration

Co-operation partners and where they are, sources of knowledge, clusters and associations, and barriers to collaboration.

Digitalisation and technology adoption

Technologies and digital tools in use, online sales, investment, skills training, generative AI, and what drives or blocks adoption.

Innovation, R&D and intellectual property

Product and process innovation, R&D and innovation spending and staff, barriers, public support, intellectual property and licensing.

Technology readiness level

Where the firm’s main technology sits on the nine-level TRL scale, and the level it aims to reach.

Energy efficiency

Energy use and costs, renewable energy, energy management, audits and certificates, efficiency investments and savings.

Business finance

Equity and debt finance, public financial support, and whether each was used for R&D or innovation.

Business support and training

Applications to and participation in business support programmes, and formal training for staff.

Management practices

Problem solving, performance monitoring, targets, manager bonuses, promotion and dealing with underperformance.

Competition

Main market, number of competitors, changes in competition and the ability to raise prices.

How to use the questions

The questions are suggestions and a starting point, not a complete or prescribed questionnaire: they do not cover every topic an evaluation may need, and they do not have to be used exactly as written.

  1. Open the topic most relevant to your evaluation, and browse the questions and response options (where applicable).
  2. Select the questions that match the indicators in your Theory of Change. The indicator repository helps define them.
  3. Adapt the wording, response options, units and reference periods to your intervention and regional context.

The source surveys below give more detail and many more questions, so it is worth consulting them directly when a topic is central to your evaluation.

Sources

Key reference sources include:

  • Community Innovation Survey (CIS): the standard EU framework for measuring innovation activity in firms
  • Firm Adoption of Technology (FAT) Survey: World Bank instrument on technology adoption across sectors
  • World Bank Enterprise Surveys (WBES): firm-level outcomes including investment, productivity, and the business environment
  • World Management Survey (WMS): management practices across firms and countries
  • Flash Eurobarometer on SME Digitalisation: digital technology adoption and skills across EU member states
  • ECB Survey on Access to Finance of Enterprises (SAFE): financing conditions and constraints for SMEs
