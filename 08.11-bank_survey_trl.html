Survey questions: technology readiness level

Survey question repository

Matching indicators: innovation and intellectual property

What is the current Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of your firm’s main technology or product under development? Please select the description that best matches the current state of your technology:

  • TRL 1 - Basic principles observed: Scientific research has begun; basic properties of the technology have been observed and reported
  • TRL 2 - Technology concept formulated: Application of the technology has been conceived; no experimental proof yet
  • TRL 3 - Experimental proof of concept: Active research and development has begun; first laboratory studies completed
  • TRL 4 - Technology validated in laboratory: The technology has been validated in a laboratory environment
  • TRL 5 - Technology validated in relevant environment: The technology has been validated in an environment relevant to its intended application (e.g. industrial simulation)
  • TRL 6 - Technology demonstrated in relevant environment: A prototype or pilot system has been demonstrated in a relevant environment
  • TRL 7 - System prototype demonstration in operational environment: The prototype is close to the planned operational system and has been demonstrated in an operational environment
  • TRL 8 - System complete and qualified: The technology has been proven to work in its final form and under expected conditions
  • TRL 9 - Actual system proven in operational environment: The technology is fully mature and has been proven in its final form through successful operation

What is the target TRL your firm aims to reach for this technology within the next [two / three] years? (Select from the same TRL 1-9 scale above)

A note on TRL for managing authorities: The TRL scale was originally developed by NASA and adapted by the European Commission for use across all Horizon Europe and ERDF R&D instruments. TRL 1-3 corresponds to basic and applied research; TRL 4-6 to technology development and validation; TRL 7-9 to demonstration and market deployment. Many EU grant schemes specify a minimum entry TRL (e.g. TRL 4 or above) and a target exit TRL, making TRL advancement a natural primary outcome for R&D support evaluations. Self-reported TRL should ideally be validated against objective evidence (e.g. prototype documentation, test reports) where possible.
